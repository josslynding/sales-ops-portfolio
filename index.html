The Framework

A three-layer operating system connecting executive visibility, tactical deal management, and complex transaction execution. Applicable across manufacturing, industrial equipment, professional services, and any complex B2B environment.

Layer 1: Executive Visibility Layer

Unified Revenue Dashboard

  • Single-source weekly scorecard consolidating marketing, BD, and sales metrics
  • Real-time pipeline coverage, forecast accuracy, and funnel health tracking
  • 3-minute quarterly progress assessment for executive leadership
  • Regional, product line, and customer segment drill-down capabilities

Problem Solved: Eliminated conflicting reports requiring days of manual reconciliation

Layer 2: Deal Qualification & Inspection Layer

Evidence-Based Sales Process

  • Stage-gate methodology with clear entry/exit criteria for each phase
  • MEDDPICC/BANT qualification frameworks converted to inspection checklists
  • Standardized templates: deal inspection cards, mutual action plans, risk assessments
  • Automated red flag detection (14 rules) with severity-based escalation
  • Weekly cadence: Monday identification → Friday resolution

Problem Solved: Sales teams no longer rely on "gut feel"—every forecast is evidence-backed

Layer 3: Complex Deal Acceleration

Milestone-Based Transaction Management

  • 5-milestone framework: Qualification → Solution Design → Negotiation → Approval → Handoff
  • Standard blocker taxonomy (8 categories) with resolution playbooks
  • Control Tower for high-value, multi-stakeholder deals ($500K+ ACV)
  • Cross-functional coordination framework (Sales, Engineering, Finance, Legal)

Problem Solved: Complex deals progress predictably instead of stalling without visibility

Core Methodologies

MEDDPICC Qualification Framework

Converted the 8-element methodology into actionable inspection cards:

  • Metrics: Quantified business value and ROI
  • Economic Buyer: Budget authority identification
  • Decision Criteria/Process: Evaluation rubric
  • Paper Process: Procurement workflow mapping
  • Identify Pain: Specific business problems
  • Champion: Internal advocate validation
  • Competition: Competitive positioning

Commit Approval Gate: 0 Red Flags + Max 1 Yellow

Evidence-Driven Forecasting

Replaced subjective forecasts with evidence-based categorization:

  • Commit: All 7 evidence points validated (90%+ confidence)
  • Best Case: 1 critical condition pending (e.g., budget approval)
  • Upside: Active but timeline uncertain
  • Pipeline: Early qualification phase

No probability guessing—explicit entry criteria for each category.

Automated Risk Detection

14-rule system for proactive deal health monitoring:

  • 🔴 High: 24-72h response SLA (e.g., Champion unreachable)
  • 🟡 Medium: 1-week action plan (e.g., Budget pending)
  • 🟢 Low: Monthly review (e.g., Deal age 2× avg cycle)

Auto-escalation to management when SLAs breach
